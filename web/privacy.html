Privacy policy

Asr is built around a simple boundary: raw device-usage data stays on your phone. Only the information needed to run your account, pact, and witness updates is sent to the server.

Last updated 3 September 2026

Information Asr receives

The Asr server receives and stores:

  • Your account email and name.
  • Your date of birth, country, and gender, if you choose to provide them.
  • The app limits you set.
  • Whether you kept or broke your limits each day.
  • Daily minutes for each app you chose to limit.
  • Device push tokens, so pact and witness notifications can be delivered.
  • Periodic heartbeats used to detect when Asr has been uninstalled.

What stays on your phone

Your raw usage data stays on your device. Asr does not send the server a complete record of how you use every app. It sends daily minutes only for apps you chose to limit.

Why this information is used

Asr uses this information to provide your account, apply the limits you chose, record whether a day was kept or broken, detect an uninstall, deliver notifications, and keep the witnesses you named informed about your pact.

Sharing

Your witnesses receive information about whether you kept or broke your pact and whether protection was disabled or Asr was uninstalled. Asr does not tell witnesses your raw device-usage history.

Retention and deletion

You can ask for your account and associated server data to be deleted. Send the request from the email address on your Asr account using the support contact provided with your access to the app, and state that you want your Asr account deleted. This contact is supplied directly while Asr is not yet publicly available. Information held only on your phone can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it.

Your choices

Date of birth, country, and gender are optional. You choose which apps to limit, what limits to set, and whom to name as witnesses.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new “last updated” date.
