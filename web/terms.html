Using Asr

You may use Asr to set app limits, make a time-limited pact, and share the outcome with witnesses you choose. Give Asr accurate account information, keep access to your account secure, and use the service only lawfully.

Your witnesses

Only name someone as a witness if you are comfortable sharing your pact outcomes with them. Asr may tell your witnesses whether you kept or broke the pact and whether protection was disabled or the app was uninstalled.

No guarantee of outcomes

Asr is an accountability tool, not a promise that you will meet a limit or achieve a particular health, productivity, or wellbeing result. Device settings, operating-system behavior, connectivity, and other technical conditions can affect how the service works.

Subscriptions

If Asr offers a paid subscription, it is billed and managed through Google Play, not billed directly by Asr. Google Play’s payment terms apply to purchases, renewals, cancellations, and refunds.

Acceptable use

Do not interfere with the service, attempt to access another person’s account or data, misuse witness invitations, or use Asr to harass anyone.

Availability and changes

Asr is not published yet. Features may change before release. After release, the service may be updated, interrupted, or discontinued. Material changes to these terms will be reflected here with an updated date.

Ending use

You may stop using Asr at any time. You may also request deletion of your account as described in the privacy policy.
